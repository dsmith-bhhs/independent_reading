Independent Reading
Grade 8
Students entering grade 8 are expected to read at least two independent reading books. Students are
encouraged to choose from the suggested titles on the pages that follow. In the event that a student
wishes to make an alternate selection, we ask that families take an active role in helping students select
an appropriate independent reading title.
For other suggestions, please check out YALSA’s book awards and book lists for a selection of award-
winning titles.
Note: Students will discuss their independent reading selections in September—There is no written
requirement.

The Alchemist

The Alchemist is the magical story of Santiago, an Andalusian
shepherd boy who yearns to travel in search of a worldly
