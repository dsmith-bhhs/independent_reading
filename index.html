Independent Reading

What's expected

Read a minimum of two books over the summer. You'll talk about them in September — there's nothing to write.

Summer is the starting line, not the finish — come back whenever you need your next book.

Choosing your own

You're welcome to read something that isn't on our lists. When you do, choose it together with a family member — and talk it over with a teacher or librarian.
Why we do this

While shared texts typically populate the landscape of the secondary English classroom, recreational reading plays an equally important role in developing critical thinking, empathy, and self-awareness.

During the academic year, students make quarterly independent reading selections. Over the summer, students should continue to immerse themselves in high-interest reading material.
